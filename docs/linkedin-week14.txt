I extended my Kathmandu air-quality MLOps project with an LLM observability layer.

The assistant now has versioned prompts, query traces, embedding drift checks,
and sampled offline Ragas evaluation. This week's addition is a shadow-only
challenger: replay a production question with the same context, keep both answers,
and compare their scores without serving the challenger to the user.

The Grafana dashboard brings together prompt metadata, drift, answer evaluation,
paired score differences, sample counts, and evaluation freshness.

Two lessons stood out:

1. A successful process exit does not guarantee a successful evaluation. Failed
judgments must not advance the processing watermark and silently skip traffic.

2. Relevant context is not necessarily sufficient context. An AQI measurement
can be relevant to a health question without supporting every claim in an answer.
That is why low faithfulness needs investigation, not an automatic explanation.

This is a portfolio prototype, not a claim of production-grade medical advice.
The challenger stays offline, canary traffic remains at zero, and promotion still
requires human review and stronger evaluation evidence.
